Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mann Ki Gita ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit mannkigita.com / lp.mannkigita.com and register for our paid online masterclass. By using the site and registering, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Details you provide: your name and mobile number, submitted through the registration form.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment gateway, Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details on our servers — Razorpay handles them securely. We receive only the payment status and a payment reference ID.
  • Usage & device data: standard analytics and advertising data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device/browser type, and cookies/identifiers, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel.

2. How we use your information

  • To confirm your registration and deliver the masterclass joining link (via WhatsApp/SMS).
  • To send you class reminders, updates, and related offers.
  • To process payments and issue confirmations or refunds where applicable.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising (including conversion measurement with Meta).

3. Cookies & advertising

We use cookies and the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use the site and to measure ad performance. This helps us show relevant ads and improve the experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings. We honour standard opt-out signals where technically feasible.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, specifically: Razorpay (payments), Meta (advertising/measurement), and WhatsApp/messaging providers used to deliver your joining link. These providers process data under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep your registration and payment records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, and support you. You may ask us to delete your data (subject to records we must retain by law).

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may opt out of marketing messages at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Our masterclass is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
